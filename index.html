About

I'm a PMP-certified Senior Project Manager and Technical Program Manager based in Philadelphia with over a decade of experience owning end-to-end delivery of enterprise-scale programs. My work spans Fortune 100 energy companies, payments infrastructure, and regulated healthcare environments — and the through-line is the same across all of them: delivering complex, cross-functional work with transparency, precision, and no surprises at the finish line.

I'm as comfortable presenting risk-weighted options to an executive steering committee as I am facilitating a sprint retrospective with a development team. I don't just report status — I surface problems early, structure the tradeoffs, and equip leaders to make confident decisions. At PECO Energy, I designed the governance framework that routes 50+ projects annually through stage-gate oversight. At Datacap Systems, I maintained a 95% on-time delivery rate across 15+ concurrent fintech integration projects.

I'm currently open to full-time employment and contract engagements. If you're building something complex and need someone who can own delivery end-to-end, I'd like to talk.
